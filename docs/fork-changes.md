# What this fork changes

Every change this modified version makes to [winters27/octo](https://github.com/winters27/octo),
one row each: what upstream does, what this version does instead, and the settings, endpoints
and files involved. Upstream's own changes are merged in as they come; a row goes when upstream
takes the same change over (as it did with the cover badge and the busy-LRCLIB fix in 2026.10.21).

| Change | Upstream | This version |
|---|---|---|
| Deezer as a source of audio | Deezer was the catalog only. Outside songs played from YouTube; hearts came from Soulseek, YouTube or Lidarr. | A `Deezer` settings section (`Arl`, `ArlFallback`, `Quality`, `StreamQuality`), `Subsonic:StreamSource` (`YouTube` default, `Deezer`), and `Deezer` as a value of `DownloadSource` and of a `HeartDownloadSources` step. With no ARL set, nothing plays or downloads differently. What does differ with the defaults: `GET /api/admin/settings` and `/raw-config` carry `Subsonic.StreamSource`, a `Deezer` section and a fourth (off) `Deezer` step in `HeartDownloadSources`; `/config-sources` lists five more keys; `/status` has a `deezer` service; the dashboard has a Deezer page. The `Deezer` section also holds the playback cache's `CacheEnabled` (off), `CachePath`, `CacheMaxGiB` and `CacheRetentionDays`, with `GET` and `DELETE /api/admin/deezer/cache` for what it holds. |
| Family: a heart on a shared song the member cannot see | A member who is not given a shared library hearted an outside row for a song that library holds: the heart was answered "already in the library; favoriting it instead of downloading", on a song they cannot play, and nothing reached their own library. | The heart goes down the chain: the download finds the shared copy and downloads nothing, and the family pool gives the member a copy of their own, linked into their library, as it already did when their heart joined a download in progress. A member who is given that library still gets a favorite and no copy. |
| Better quality from Deezer | `LibraryActions:UpgradeSource` is `Auto`, `Soulseek` or `Lidarr`; Automatic asks Soulseek, then Lidarr. | A fourth value, `Deezer`. With a Deezer account set, Automatic asks Deezer first, then Soulseek, then Lidarr; without one it is unchanged. Deezer is asked for FLAC and nothing lesser (`FLAC_ONLY`), so an account given no FLAC fetches nothing and the next source is asked. The "no source set up" and gate texts name Deezer too. |
| Find songs lists Deezer's copy | A look searches Soulseek and Lidarr. | With a Deezer account set, a look has a third source, `Deezer`, listed first: one copy, the catalog's track for the artist and title, with the format the account is given and the file's size (read from a two-byte range request). For a library song only a FLAC is listed. A picked copy is fetched from Deezer; where first choices tie the order is Deezer, Soulseek, Lidarr. |
| Family: a member adopted under a capitalised name | A member brought in from an existing Navidrome account keeps its spelling ("Richard"); their folder is made lower case. The pool refused the name ("is not a family member") and built paths with the capital, so their downloads stayed in the pool. | Every path to a member's folder is built from the lower-case name (`pool::folder_name`), and the pool accepts the member. |
| Radio warm-up with streams off | The warm-up worker ran with `ExposeRadioAsStreams` off, found no station to resolve and warned "0/N stations; will retry" every minute. | It skips the listener while radio or its streams are off. |
| Dashboard: Reset history did nothing | The Radio page's "Reset history" handler read `event.currentTarget` after awaiting the confirmation, when it is null, so it threw and the DELETE was never sent. | The button is read before the first await (same in Disconnect Spotify and Connect Spotify's error note). |
| Starter Radio switch | A listener with fewer learned plays than `MinimumPlays` gets Starter Radio from whatever plays there are, down to one, or from random library songs. | `LastFm:EnableStarterRadio` (default true: unchanged). Off, no Starter Radio is built or shown, random songs are not seeded, and a listener who had only a Starter loses it at the next refresh. The bundled compose file sets `LASTFM_ENABLE_STARTER_RADIO` to false. |
| Deezer account health | None. | `GET /api/admin/deezer/account[?fresh=true]`: per account whether it signs in, `refused` or `unreachable`, the plan and whether it gives FLAC and 320 kbps, with notes when the plan gives less than is asked for; the status probe uses the same verdict; a `DeezerAccountWatch` worker checks every 30 minutes and logs when an account fails or returns. The check reuses a sign-in a play is using (only `fresh=true` signs in for it); a refused ARL is reported without asking Deezer until it changes or is checked fresh. |
| Soulseek switched off | `Soulseek:Enabled` existed and hearts skipped Soulseek when off, but Better quality and Find songs still counted it as a source whenever slskd's address and sign-in were set, the compose file did not pass the switch, the dashboard had no switch, and the installer always asked for a Soulseek account. | Better quality and Find songs leave Soulseek out while it is off (Find songs says "Soulseek is switched off on this server"); `SOULSEEK_ENABLED` in compose and `.env.example`; a "Use Soulseek" switch on the Soulseek page (restart). `Soulseek:Enabled` is a restart key. |
| YouTube switched off | YouTube could not be switched off: the yt-dlp shim was always asked (search-time video lookups, prewarm, stream sizes, playback, hearts) and YouTube Music fed radio whenever its own switch was on. | `YouTube:Enabled` (`YOUTUBE_ENABLED`, raw like `YouTube:ShimUrl`, default on; a restart key). Off: `YouTubeResolver` never calls the shim (every lookup finds nothing, every stream and download is unavailable), the YouTube Music client is unconfigured, hearts skip YouTube steps, the status card says "Off. Optional.", and outside songs play from Deezer whatever `StreamSource` says when an ARL is set (`AppSettings::deezer_stream_when`). "Use YouTube" switch on Streams & hearts. |
| No installer, no bundled Navidrome (2026-10-12) | `install.sh` set up `.env` interactively (tested by `scripts/test-install.sh` in CI), and `docker-compose.yml` had a `navidrome-starter` service behind the `navidrome` profile for anyone without a music server. | Both removed: setup is `cp .env.example .env`, edit, `docker compose up -d`, against a Navidrome of your own. The starter-stack block left `.env.example` (`COMPOSE_PROFILES`, `NAVIDROME_ADMIN_PASSWORD`, `NAVIDROME_PORT`, `NAVIDROME_DATA_DIR`). The update helper (`scripts/updater/`) is unchanged and never called the installer. The C# tree still mentions `install.sh` in comments. |
| Deezer CDN reads give up when idle | (New with the Deezer backend.) The stream client has no overall timeout, so a CDN connection that stalled mid-song left a download, a cache fill or a play waiting forever. | Each read of the CDN body fails after 60 seconds with nothing arriving (`DeezerResolver::BODY_IDLE_TIMEOUT`, `idle_limited`); the time only runs while a reader asks for more, so a paused player is not cut off. |
| A download waiting for a library job says so | A finished download that had to wait for a library job holding the music folder (the cover upgrade, Tidy artists, a re-tag) showed only "Importing" ("Adding to library") until the job ended, which read as a hang. | The row's note says "Waiting for the cover upgrade to finish" (the job's name), and the log says so once. |
| Private fork images | `docker-compose.yml` pulls `ghcr.io/sleezy6/*`; the image workflow builds amd64 only, also on pushes to `feat/deezer-backend`, and checks compose against this repo's own owner. The update check is off by default, since a private repo's releases can't be read without signing in. |
| Lengths from Deezer when outside songs play from Deezer | A row that lacks a length is given Deezer's catalog length, never a YouTube video's, when outside songs stream from Deezer (read once at startup). |
| A station waits up to 3 s for its songs' lengths | A station (`getPlaylist`, the native station tracks) and `getSimilarSongs` wait up to 3 seconds for the Deezer length lookups they queue, so songs go out with a length. Before, a station never waited, and with YouTube off nothing else filled lengths in, so iPhone players showed `--:--`. |
| A Deezer seek opens one CDN connection | A range that starts inside the song asks the CDN for its stripe at once and reads the song's size from the answer's `Content-Range` (a 416 gives it too). It used to open the whole song first only to learn the size. Ranges from the first stripe or counted from the end still read the size first. |
| A station looks every row's length up, four at a time | Within its wait, a station (and `getSimilarSongs`) looks up the length of every row it has none for, four at a time and in list order, rather than up to 20 rows one after another; what is not back in time goes on in the background. |
| With YouTube off, a song Deezer cannot serve says so | The log no longer says "playing it from YouTube" when YouTube is switched off: it says the song cannot play, and the YouTube step is skipped. |
| Song lengths from Apple's catalog | A song without a length is asked of Deezer, then Apple's catalog (the iTunes Search API, ISRC first, then artist and title, by the same recording rules; paced with the cover-art requests), then Last.fm, then the video. Apple's lengths rank between Last.fm's and Deezer's and are saved as Last.fm's number with `ShownFromApple`. Off in the test app. |
| Library stubs listed as the whole album | Since PR #92, search and getArtist listed a library album holding under half of a catalog album beside the catalog album, and any counted catalog album passed the by-name filter. | A library album holding half or more of a catalog album (by Navidrome's songCount against the catalog's) hides it, counted or not; one holding less is left out and the catalog album listed in its place, with its owned count. Without both counts the library album hides it, as before PR #92. |
| Songs found by ISRC when Deezer's search misses them | A song Deezer has but its search does not list (a copy not sold in the server's country, an artist sharing a name) played from YouTube. | After the track search, album tracklist and top songs all miss, `DeezerMetadataService` asks an `IsrcSource` (MusicBrainz, `isrcs_for`: same title, agreeing credit, within 10 s of the song's length when known) for up to three ISRCs and looks each up with `/track/isrc:`. The length given is never a video's (`lookup_seconds`). Every artist-name lookup reads 25 of Deezer's artists (`ARTIST_NAME_CANDIDATES`), one shared cached search, and a search row's settled namesake gives way to one with more fans. |
| Favorites loved on Last.fm | None. | `LastFm:LoveFavorites` (dashboard, on by default): a connected listener's star on a song is `track.love`, an unstar `track.unlove`, through the scrobbling session, only for a sign-in Navidrome accepted; the favorites they had are loved once after their next play (`lastfm-loves.json` keeps the session that was done for). + Add from the Octo apps (`c=Octo`, an outside song) is not a favorite and is not loved. `/api/admin/lastfm/scrobble` carries `loveFavorites` and each user's `loves`. |
| Sounds alike from Navidrome | None. | `RadioSources:SoundsAlikeFrom` (`octo-sonic`, the default, or `navidrome`): with `navidrome`, the Sounds alike source asks Navidrome's `getSimilarSongs` (the listener's sign-in; a station refresh Octo's admin sign-in) and octo-sonic's sound check no longer reorders radio's library picks. One more config-sources row. |
| Lyrics Octo did not write may be replaced | Only lyrics marked `[re:Octo]` were ever replaced. | `Metadata:ReplaceOthersLyrics` (dashboard, off by default): on, an upgrade walk's better lyrics and lyrics picked under Better lyrics or Fix a song's lyrics replace a song's own `.lrc`, `.txt` or embedded lyrics too, each recorded in the undo journal; Undo still puts lyrics back only while Octo's are there; `.ttml` and the like still keep Octo out. One more config-sources row. |
| YouTube fallback plays only the song | yt-dlp's closest-length result played whatever it was; without a known length the first result did, and a 59 s sample's length was then kept for the song. | Both YouTube searches get the song's known length (`lookup_seconds`, never a video's); yt-dlp's result plays only within 10 s of it and never when its title says preview, snippet, teaser or sample (`is_the_song`); otherwise nothing plays. |
| Deezer track id kept on the routing | Every play, heart and Find songs looked the song up in Deezer's catalog again (from the in-memory cache when it was still there). | A Deezer length lookup whose hit names the very recording asked for (`recording_matches`, not marked unreadable) keeps its id on the routing (`DeezerTrackId` in `external-ids.json`, written only when known), as does the id a play's own search found; plays, hearts and downloads use it without searching. No audio under a kept id forgets it and searches once more (a download asking for lossless only never forgets it). |
| Deezer requests counted | None. | `GET /api/admin/deezer/requests` (last hour and since start, by purpose and lane, requests the rate limiter turned away, plays that needed no search), shown on the dashboard's Deezer page; an hourly `Deezer requests in the last hour` log line. The purpose is marked by the code that asked (`DeezerUse::scope`), else read from the API path. |
| Artist bans for radio | Only songs rated one star were kept off a listener's radio; their artist was only weighed down. | `setRating` 1 on an artist (library or outside) bans the artist from that listener's song radio, stations and mixes, matched by `artist_key`; `LastFm:ArtistBanAfter` (default 3, 0 off) songs rated one star do the same. Any other rating on the artist, or `DELETE /api/admin/lastfm/radio/artist-ban?user=&artist=`, lifts it, an automatic ban until another song is rated one star. `ArtistBans` in `lastfm-radio-state.json` (left out while empty); `/api/admin/lastfm/radio` adds `artistBanAfter` and `bannedArtists`. Radio seeded by the banned artist still plays them. |
| Explicit choice per listener | `Subsonic:ExplicitFilter` for everyone, with clean only for a kid profile. | `PUT /api/family/me/explicit` (`Server`, `All`, `CleanOnly`, `ExplicitOnly`), `explicit` on `PUT /api/family/members/{name}`, `/api/family/me` and member JSON; kept as `ExplicitChoices` in `family.json`. Octo-built lists follow it (a kid profile stays clean); while Family is on, answers on the kid-filter endpoints drop explicit songs (`CleanOnly`, unmarked kept) or clean edits (`ExplicitOnly`). |
| Family playlists shared with chosen members | Every member and the owner saw every family playlist. | `SharedWith` (left out while empty) limits a playlist to those members, its maker, the owner and family managers, in the family API and every Subsonic path; set by its maker or a manager with `sharedWith` on `POST` or `PUT /api/family/playlists/{id}`; `GET /api/family/playlists/people` lists who can be chosen (family only). Summaries add `sharedWith` and `mayShare`. |
| Apple album id by MusicBrainz barcode | `album_collection_id` searched by name only; Apple's search omits an explicit album with no clean edition, and `same_text` took "Olympia (Remixes)" for "Olympia" (a subtitle on one side only lowered confidence). | When the search names no match, `BarcodeSource` (MusicBrainz `release/?query=release:… AND artist:… AND status:official`, digital releases first, six hours cached, background lane) gives up to six barcode forms for one `lookup?upc=`. Album titles are matched with `extras_must_agree`, for the id and the master cover alike. |
| SpotiFLAC in Better quality | SpotiFLAC rides on the Soulseek step for hearts and downloads only; `UpgradeSources` plans the Soulseek step only when slskd is set up, and drops it while slskd is not logged in. | The Soulseek step counts as set up, and is never out, while SpotiFLAC is on (`UpgradeSources::set_up`, `available`, the executor's `soulseek_out`); `name()` says "SpotiFLAC" or "Soulseek or SpotiFLAC". The step's download is upstream's `download_lossless`, in SpotiFLAC's placement order. |
| Deezer radio source | Song radio's sources were Last.fm, YouTube Music, ListenBrainz and Sounds alike. | A `deezer` radio provider ("Deezer"): the song's Deezer track mix (`song.getSearchTrackMix` on Deezer's web gateway, with an anonymous session from `deezer.getUserData`), else the exact-name artist's `/artist/{id}/radio`; both kept 24 hours. Each suggestion carries its Deezer track id (`SimilarTrack::deezer_track_id`, `DeezerTrackId` on a station's track in `lastfm-radio-state.json`, written only when known), kept on the song's routing, so a play or heart from Deezer's radio needs no search. `RadioSources:Deezer` (`RADIO_DEEZER`, on) and `RadioSources:DeezerWeight` (1.0); its requests count as `radio` on the Deezer requests card. Ported from am-yy/octo. |
| Song radio drawn, not cut | Sources were asked for as many songs as the radio wanted; a song several sources named scored the sum; the top of the blend was served, in the same order every time; Octo apps got up to 100 songs and other apps 50. | Each source is asked for 100; a song scores its best source plus a quarter of the others; the rank discount halves at 40 instead of 20. The best pick leads, the rest are drawn by weight (Efraimidis–Spirakis), a song served to the listener in the last 7 days at 0.35 (kept in memory, up to 3000 per listener); an artist takes at most 10% (the seed's artist 25%) and songs played in the last 8 days go to the back. Up to 100 songs for every app; Octo apps get them sorted by score. Library-led radio is unchanged. Ported from am-yy/octo. |
| Family playlist covers | A family playlist had no `coverArt`; `getCoverArt` for its id fell through to Navidrome, which does not know it. | `coverArt` (`<id>_<tag>`, the tag changing with its picture, name, song count and newest addition) on family playlists in `getPlaylists`, `getPlaylist` and `createFamilyPlaylist`. `getCoverArt` paints it as a mix's (kind `family`, "Playlist", colored by the first four albums the caller is shown), only for someone it is shared with. `setFamilyPlaylistCover` (POST; `id` in the query; the picture as the body with an `image/*` type or a multipart `image`/`file` part; JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF or BMP, up to 20 MB, at least 100 px a side) and `deleteFamilyPlaylistCover`, for whoever may rename it, answer `familyPlaylistCover {id, coverArt}`; the picture is kept as its center square, at most 1500 px, at `<config>/covers/<id>.jpg`, and goes with the playlist. `octoFamily` version 2. |
